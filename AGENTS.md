# Project conventions

- Keep public Mira demonstration styles and color tokens scoped to `.mira-page`; this prevents presentation changes from affecting school and account screens.
- Render the Mira EJS view directly in Vite's development preview; production continues to use its existing Express controller so preview content cannot drift from the actual page.
- Use Vite only when development is invoked with port 8080; ordinary local development retains both Express servers to preserve the application's existing workflow.
- In Vite's static homepage preview, render the shared EJS footer instead of its outdated exported footer so shared footer changes remain visible without duplicating presentation code.
- Keep workspace rail alignment in the shared workspace stylesheet so Settings, Reports, Admissions and Management use the same collapsed control positioning.
- Keep Report Builder data and initialization scripts inside its page root, scope listeners to that element and bind once per mounted page; initialization should not depend on a particular layout wrapper.
- Save and export Report Builder's last successful request, not subsequently edited criteria; this keeps those actions consistent with the displayed results.- Decide Management, Reports and Admissions menu items in `utils/workspaceAccess.js`, and mirror each item's page guard there; the hamburger and hub pages both read it so a menu never offers a page that would redirect the person away.
- A person's saved permission rows are authoritative; role defaults apply only to someone never saved (`effectivePermissionsForRole`), so unticking a permission really removes it.
- Define permissions in `PERMISSION_OPTIONS` (group, description, `highRisk`, `parent`); the employee form renders from it, and a follow-up counts only while its `parent` is held. PayGira rows saved before follow-ups existed are expanded in `withLegacyPaygira`.
- Plan features are enforced centrally: `userHasPermission` refuses anything outside the school's plan for everyone, including owners, and `permissionOptionsFor` hides it from forms. Tie a permission to a plan with `PERMISSION_FEATURES` or its option's `features`; don't add a separate check.
- Plans are one list, `utils/planCatalog.js` (released by `plan_catalog`), and a ladder: Starter, Pro, Premium, each with everything of the one before. CORE is in every plan even with nothing ticked (`withCore` adds it wherever plan features are read); PLAN items are ticked per plan in Admin › Pricing, grouped by the plan that first has them; an item with `addon` (the parent and student portals, in every plan) means a school on that plan switches the add-on on in Settings › Integrations at no charge (`includedAddons`, `orgPricing.schoolBill`), and schools on other plans buy it. A new module goes in the catalog. Plan pages list only what a plan adds to the one before it (`ladder`: "Everything in Starter, plus"). PayGira is in every plan.
- The new plan page shows everywhere in development and, in production, where `plan_page` is released (`planPageOn`); everyone else gets `servicePlanClassic.ejs`, the page as it was (delete it once `plan_page` is released to everyone).
- The plan page a new school sees (`views/account/servicePlan.ejs`) says each thing once: what every plan shares (the free trial, the students included) in the header, on a card only its own price, what an extra student costs and what it adds to the plan before it ("Everything in Pro, plus", opening to a quiet block that lists Starter's features, then Pro's), Most popular on `planCatalog.POPULAR_PLAN`, whole-dollar prices, and one line under "How often to pay" for students above the included ones. Plan lists in Settings and on the change-plan page are the same ladder. It is a public page: a visitor belongs to no school, so a `selected` release never reaches it.
- A billing cycle's "active" means offered to new choices. A school already paying on a cycle keeps it after it stops being offered (`resolveBillingCycle`, `getDynamicCycleByKey`); Monthly, 3 Months, 6 Months and 1 Year (10% off) are offered; schools pay on each of them, so don't take one off without checking who is on it.
- The United States (default) price list is per month: $39 / $59 / $79 with 20 students included, then $2.00 / $2.40 / $2.80 for each extra student a month.
- Every add-on price is a month's price, for schools and hubs: a hub bill covering several months charges each month (`computeBill`), and a manual add-on charge is filled in for the whole bill. A school's Billing card lists its plan, each add-on it uses (with its monthly cost, free, or "included in your plan") and the month's total. A price list whose "months in a term" is 1 prices extra students per month; wording follows it ("a month" or "per term").
- Keep `utils/processGuards.js` installed first in every long-running process: Node exits with status 1 on an unhandled rejection otherwise. Keep lists keyed by visitors or schools in `BoundedMap`, and uploads behind `uploadGate`; the database has only 64 MB of shared buffers, so raise pool sizes only with `DB_POOL_MAX`.
- A driver holds only `driverPermissions`: Driver and Transportation, plus Users / Employees once they are a lead. `isAuthenticated` lets them reach only the pages `DRIVER_PAGES` ties to what they hold, and nothing else.
- A driver's report stays between them and the leads until a lead shares it (`utils/transportReports.js`); only reports about a student can be shared with parents or teachers, and teachers also need Class Bus Reports.
- Only a manager can change a manager, and only a manager can change their own account through the staff pages. Check `staffChangeRefusal` on every page that edits, holds, removes or restores staff.
- A driver with Report Builder sees their own rows; a report's `fullAccess` permission opens everyone's (`ownerWhere`, `ownRowsFor`).
- Read and write live bus positions through `utils/liveBus.js`: drivers and viewers share an in-memory position, with one database write per 15 seconds per trip and one read per few seconds per school, so viewers add no database load.
- Ending a trip never makes a driver wait: `endTrip` closes the screen in the same tap and `sendEnd` delivers the last answers and the end in one safe-to-repeat request, retried until it lands (`managira-trip-ending` in the phone's storage). Keep `saveArrivals` one statement.
- The parent's tracker measures along the route's real road through its stops, in order (`setRoad`, `progressOnRoad`), never a direct line from the bus to a stop. An estimate is only made for a bus on its route, pauses at stops, is marked, and never says the bus passed anyone.
- Only the map zooms on the driver's page (`pageZoom`, `html.drv-nozoom`).
- Read and write boarding and who's riding today through `utils/transportBoarding.js`: viewers of a school share one read per few seconds. A driver only marks exceptions: a bus driving off a stop counts as all boarded (saved as `auto`), and ending a trip counts everyone on board as arrived.
- In auth middleware, pass database-busy errors to `next(error)` (`isDatabaseUnavailable`): a busy database must show the "try again" page, never sign anyone out. Don't raise `DB_POOL_MAX` above 15 to handle load: at 2,000 active users, 30 was measurably slower.
- Put new table changes in `database/migrations/*.sql` (run by `npm run migrate`), written to be safe to repeat, not in code that runs while pages load.
- Shards are in `docs/SHARDS.md`. Every table must be classified in `utils/shards/schoolTables.js`, or every move stops. Write links with `via(...)`: a bare column would match every school's rows.
- `/admin/shards` is for platform super admins with a fresh password (`middleware/shardAdminGuard.js`); record each action with `registry.audit`.
- Every new feature goes in `utils/shards/features.js` with `access: "selected"` and is wrapped with `isOn` (`featureOn` in views): a push must reach only the sandbox shard until it's released from `/admin/shards/features`.
- `sandbox-shard` shares the main shard's database. A move between them changes only the directory; never let `moveSchool` run on one database, because it ends by deleting the old copy.
- A test shard gets nothing of production's: no control database, keys or shared secrets (`environmentFor`).
- Single sign-on lives in `utils/sso/`. One connection per school; an answer can only ever sign in an account of that school, never a platform admin. A connection stays off until a real test passes and a manager confirms their password, and changing how it signs in turns it off again. A new session field on a password sign-in must also go in `utils/sso/signIn.js`.
- A school's main manager can always use a password (`passwordRefused`): if a provider goes down, someone must still get in. Single sign-on opens their account only if they turned it on themselves (`turned_on_by_main`).
- Only a Manager reaches single sign-on settings, whatever permissions anyone else holds (`managersOnly`). Once it's on, saving or turning it off asks for the password, and only the protocol in use keeps its keys.
- Each member of staff signs in one way: their school's single sign-on provider or a Managira password (`users.sign_in_method`, 'sso' / 'password', empty = password; rules in `connections.waysFor`). Add user and Edit user ask "How they sign in" (`views/partials/employeeSignIn.ejs`) only while the school's connection is tested and on (`providerForStaff`), a new person starting on the provider; Settings has "Use <provider> for everyone" (`everyoneUsesSso`, asks for the password). Turning single sign-on on moves nobody, and off moves everyone back to passwords. The main manager always keeps a password (and the provider once they turned it on); students the school allows get both. Until `2026-11-21_sign_in_method.sql` has run (`methodReady`), the school's connection decides for everyone as before.
- The login page is email first (`ssoController.continueWithEmail`, `signInChoice`): after the email it shows only that person's way in: "Your school signs in with <provider>" (SAML or OpenID Connect) and its Continue button (`#ssoForm`, works without scripts), or the password box, or both for the main manager. Nothing is sent to the provider until they choose it. A right password from someone set to the provider shows that step instead; the provider answering for someone set to a password is refused; Forgot password tells someone on the provider to use it. Single sign-on has no page of its own: `/account/sso` and its messages land on the login page.
- Users / Employees shows each person's way in (`connections.staffSignIn`, the same rules as the login page) and whether their last sign-in was through the provider (`sso_events`). Invitations follow the choice (`inviteSignIn`): someone on the provider gets "Log in with <provider>" and no password link; moving someone to a password they don't have emails them the set-password link.
- From the login page, single sign-on goes to a school only if no other school has that email (`connectionForEmail`): a school can add any email to its lists. A link from another site asks first (`ssoConfirm`). Nothing looks up an email while someone types.
- Keep me logged in (`utils/keepSignedIn.js`) goes on every sign-in route: 30 days when ticked, the browser session when not, and never for platform admins.
- A right password is held for an emailed code (`utils/loginCode.js`, feature `login_code`) on every password sign-in route, outside `keepSignedInChoice`: the session it made is set aside on the server and only the right code puts it back. Single sign-on skips it, and so does an account with no email. A right code remembers that browser for the school's trust duration (3 days unless it chose otherwise), counted from the code and never pushed forward by signing in again.
- Reminders and verification codes go out from reminders@managira.com: pass `sender: "reminders"` to `sendEmailSafely`/`sendEmailInBackground`. Receipts, invoices, invitations and password emails keep the normal address.
- A page made without the app frame (`layout: false`) always opens as a page of its own: the page-switcher only swaps pages that have `#app-scale-root`.
- A link into a Management, Admissions or Reports panel (`?panel=...&page=...`) from another page carries `data-no-spa`: the page-switcher runs the new page's scripts before the address changes, so the hub would open its default panel instead. Events, announcements and activities are managed in one Management panel; the dashboard's Manage links open it there.
- Every form a visitor can send without signing in (help ticket, request a demo, job application, a school website's contact form, online admission, school sign-up) needs a person (`utils/humanCheck.js` `requireHuman(<form>)` on the route, after any upload parser; `data-human-check="<form>"` on the form and `public/js/humanCheck.js` on the page; a form sent by a script awaits `ManagiraHuman.ready(form)` and calls `renew` after). It's our own signed proof of work (no outside service, nothing to click), a hidden field, at least 3 seconds counted from the server's challenge, each answer once, and a per-network hourly limit. A confirmation emailed to an address someone typed never repeats what they typed and goes out at most 3 times a day per address (`emailAllowance`); anything typed that goes into an email is escaped (`escapeHtml`: `renderCleanEmail` prints greetings, paragraphs and detail values as given). Sign-in pages stay out of it (see `signInGuard`).
- Sign-in forms use `signInGuard`, never `authRateLimit`: nobody is ever refused and a right password always gets in. Past a free allowance, wrong tries wait their turn before the password is checked.
- Every password sign-in page includes `partials/authLoader` (the hopping logo); it only covers the screen.
- Parent pages use `isGuardianAuthenticated`. `isAuthenticated` is the staff door and refuses parents and students; a page never relies on `blockGuardians` alone.
- Student and parent ids come from other tables, so they're never looked up as staff: `userHasPermission`, `setAllowedRoles` and `setAllowedPermissions` skip them (`isPortalRole`). A school's signup email counts as its owner only for a Manager, and `takesOwnersEmail` stops anyone editing their way onto it.
- `/static` answers 404 in production: it holds a design copy for the local Vite preview, not pages.
- A parent's children are the rows of `guardian_students` (parent profile to student), nothing else. The older per-student `guardians` rows are numbered separately: never match their `guardian_id` to the signed-in parent or address a parent's notice by it (that showed one family's child to another parent).
- Inside templates `JSON.stringify` is script-safe (`utils/safeJson.js`, set as `app.locals.JSON`): keep writing `<%- JSON.stringify(x) %>` and never build JSON for a page by hand. Everything else a page prints goes through `<%= %>`; in browser code, names and titles go in with `textContent`, not `innerHTML`.
- A teacher's gradebook and report cards go through `requireClassScope` (`utils/classScope.js`, released by `teacher_class_scope`): their classes, and their subjects unless they are the class's main teacher, the same as the class dashboard offers. Put it on any new route that reads or saves a class's scores.
- A database connection taken by hand (`db.connect()`) goes back in a `finally`, on every way out: one never returned is gone for good, and 15 of them made every page answer "database unavailable" (two public hub-invitation addresses did this). Prefer `db.query`; `tools/connectAudit.py` in the local copy lists every hand-taken connection.
- Never call `req.flash` after `req.session.destroy()`: it throws outside any handler and stops the whole server. To sign someone out with a message, use `req.session.regenerate` and flash inside its callback.
- Caches of names or labels are keyed by school (`utils/activityLog.js`): the same id is another person in another school.
- Fetch an address someone typed only through `utils/safeFetch.js`: it refuses the server's own network at the moment of connecting, including after a redirect.
- Staff calendar sync is one way and only Google Calendar and Outlook: Managira makes a calendar named "Managira · <school>" in the person's account and fills it with what their own calendar page shows them (`itemsToSend`: events, activities, personal events, tasks, and classes, bus runs and birthdays for the next 8 weeks, birthdays as names without ages; never calendars shared with them; announcements aren't on the calendar at all, they live on dashboards). Syncs of one connection take turns (`waitForAdvisoryLock` on `calendar-sync:<id>`, re-reading the connection inside), and Google's or Microsoft's "slow down" and a just-made calendar's first 404s are retried (`patiently`), so a sync never stops halfway or makes a second calendar, with the same visibility as the page (`syncActor`). Nothing is read from their other calendars, so Google is asked only for `calendar.app.created` (`calendarConnect.GOOGLE_SCOPES`, never merged with Classroom's grants). It runs only in the background: within seconds of a change (`scheduleOrgSync`, called wherever events, announcements and activities are written) and from the `calendar-sync` job every 10 minutes (`runCalendarSyncSweep`); never because a page opened or a button was pressed. The Sync window shows only providers set up on the server (`providersConfigured`: Google's keys, `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID` and `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET`), each just Connected (with the account) / Reconnect needed and one button; `?sync=1` opens it once and is then dropped from the address. Share is its own window. The grid is drawn only in `public/css/calendar.css` (`#calendar ...`); don't style FullCalendar items or toolbar in `workspace.css`. `MICROSOFT_LOGIN_URL` and `MICROSOFT_GRAPH_URL` exist only to point the local copy at a stand-in. A Managira calendar deleted over there is made again with the next change. Private iCal links and subscribed calendars still work for anyone already using them but aren't offered to staff.
- The calendar shows birthdays (`utils/birthdayCalendar.js`: students in the person's classes, every class for those who run the school, and staff), bus runs only while transportation is on, and the page's flash messages (connecting a calendar reports there). Mira reads it (`get_my_calendar`), finds free time (`find_free_time`, `freeSlots`) and puts meetings on the person's own calendar (`add_to_my_calendar`, a personal event).
- Calendar sign-ins, Google Classroom tokens and secret links are stored with `lockSecret` and read with `openSecret` (older plain values still open, and Classroom's are locked the first time they're used). Classroom asks Google only for the permissions it uses (`GOOGLE_CLASSROOM_SCOPES`); each must also be listed in Google Cloud › Data Access, and the privacy policy's "Google and Microsoft Account Data" section must stay true to what the code does. Google, Outlook and subscribed calendars load through `collectOutsideItems`, never inside `collectItems`: one slow outside service must not hold up anyone's calendar.
- The main login takes parents, students and hub users to their portal only after their own sign-in has checked the password (`utils/smartLogin.js`); before that it says nothing about an email.
- A hub moves with all its schools. Only between shards on one database can a single school move alone (`wholeHub: false`); the hub then shows on each shard with the schools that are there.
- The database login the app uses can't create databases or logins (`scripts/secureDatabaseLogin.js`). The admin login acts as that login by default, so new tables stay in its reach; anything needing admin powers starts with `RESET ROLE`.
- A school's sign-in rules live in `tenant_settings` and are read through `utils/tenantSettings.js` (`settingsFor`, one read per school per minute): signed out after 24 hours unless the school chose otherwise, Keep me logged in, the verification code and remembered browsers. Only a manager changes them, in Settings, Tenant configuration (`tenant_config`), with Single sign-on under it. If the rules can't be read, nobody is signed out.
- Report cards, terminal reports, transcripts and Report Builder emails go out from reports@managira.com (`sender: "reports"`).
- Help center articles are written in code (`utils/docs/articles/*.js`, listed in `utils/docs/catalog.js` with topics and `REDIRECTS`) and synced into `documentation_articles` by slug at start-up, changing a row only when its content changed. The sync, and the help center's table setup, write only in production or on a local database (`documentationWritesAllowed`); a development server on the hosted database previews the articles from code. Visitors see `views/docs`; documentation admins keep the editor views.
- Help center screenshots (`public/images/docs`, sizes in `manifest.json`) are captured from the local test copy with sample names swapped in and restored afterwards, never from a real school.
- What parents and students see and do is documented under the Parent portal and Student portal topics (`utils/docs/articles/portals.js`). A subject that has its own guide (PayGira, the bus, pickup details, calendar sync, assessments) is linked from there, not explained twice.
- Everything crawlers and AI assistants read (public pages, structured data, `llms.txt`, the FAQ, the help center and the site chat's answers) presents Managira as a United States K-12 company: no other countries or currencies, and guides, the FAQ and the chat name only US payment and texting providers. The homepage's integrations list is the exception: it names every provider Managira connects to (Paystack, Arkesel and Africa's Talking included) and says any other platform can be added. A school's own website content is the school's. Test schools ("(TEST)" in the name, or "test" as a word of the address) stay out of the school sitemap and are marked noindex.
- Write many rows in one statement with one array per column (`unnest`), never a list of rows: a statement takes at most 65,535 values, and a big school passes that.
- A page listing a whole school (every student) sends the rows as compact JSON and builds them in the browser (`views/common/allStudents.ejs`): rendering thousands of rows on the server made one page 30 MB and blocked every other request while it rendered. A page view never writes rows it didn't change (`IS DISTINCT FROM`).
- `cacheManager.getOrFetch` refreshes an entry in the background only near the end of the lifetime it was given (`ttl`); cached values are copied for each reader, so callers may change what they get (`copy: false` only for a caller that just reads a large snapshot). Signed-in pages are `no-store`, so ETags are made only for small responses (`server.js`).
- Check and make passwords with `utils/passwordHashing.js` (bcrypt on worker threads), never `require("bcryptjs")` directly: on the main thread a morning rush of 30 sign-ins a second made each one wait 20 seconds and stalled every other page.
- A password asked for before a sensitive action (Settings' Billing & Plan, integrations, cancelling, API service accounts) is the signed-in person's own (`utils/confirmOwnPassword.js`): a member of staff's, or the school's for someone signed in as the school's own account (`accountType` "Organization"; its session keeps the school's id in `user.user_id`), never another person's or the school's for staff; a hub person who opened one of its schools (`hubOrgSwitch`) gives their own hub password and a Managira admin who opened it (`superAdminOrgSwitch`) their own admin password. Each pop-up says who is signed in (`passwordWho`). Single sign-on settings use the same check. Whoever may open the page may act with their own password. Each confirmation and wrong try goes in the activity log with what it was for (`purpose`).
- Report Builder exports take up to `exportRowsFor(format)` rows: CSV 100,000, Excel 25,000, Word 10,000. Excel and Word are built in memory; don't raise them without streaming.
- The header's styles and scripts are cached files (`public/css/header.css`, `public/js/headerMenu.js`, `public/js/headerBell.js`); keep only the school's colours inline.
- The public homepage and Mira page in the new look live in `views/home` (`landing.ejs`, `mira.ejs`); they share one head, header and footer (`views/home/partials`) and one menu list (`utils/landing.js`), styled by `public/css/landing.css`. Production shows them only once `new_landing` is released. Managira is presented as a K-12 student information system in industry wording (student information, teaching & learning, family engagement, business operations, analytics, district management), by pillars rather than a list of every feature. The homepage leads with outcomes (Skyward-style), not feature lists. Photos of people are Unsplash-licensed (`public/images/site/photo-*.webp`); app pictures (`app-*.webp`) are captured from the local test copy with sample names and the teal theme swapped in and restored afterwards, never from a real school.
- Signed-out marketing pages (not a school's own site) use the public site's header and footer (`views/home/partials`) once `newPublicLook` is on (`utils/landing.js` `isNewLook`, `_publicChrome` in `views/layout.ejs`); the app's `globalScale` zoom and global `nav`/`button`/`a` rules are countered in `public/css/landing.css`. Visitors on those pages skip the appearance recolouring (`data-appearance="none"`), so nothing waits to show. Light pages get the light header (`is-light`); the dark homepage and Mira page keep the dark one. Keep the logo at the far left and the menu and actions at the right at every width.
- Mira is for staff only (teachers, drivers, managers and every other employee): parents and students are refused in `mira/middleware/requireMiraActivated.js` and never see the header button (`middleware/setMiraStatus.js`).
- Mira works with each person's own permissions. Every tool that reads or changes school data has a rule in `mira/services/access.js` (`TOOL_ACCESS`), checked in `runTool` against `/internal/api/mira/access` (the same `userHasPermission` as the pages); tools a person can't use are never offered, unknown access refuses, and refusals name the missing permission or the school's plan.
- Mira states only figures a tool gave: `mira/services/grounding.js` sends a reply back once when a figure appears in no tool result, and tools return exact totals so Mira never counts. Tools that delete, remove or undo are offered only when the person asked for that (`destructiveAllowed`), the same call twice in one message isn't run again, and a long chat keeps a digest of its older messages.
- The parent dashboard's welcome banner shows only the parent's photo, "Hello, first name" and the inbox: no email, phone or address.
- Mira has no password form: it signs in through Managira's own page (`/account/login?next=mira`, which sends staff back to Mira's `/continue`), so every sign-in rule applies. Mira's Log out ends the shared sign-in (`/account/logout?next=mira`), and Mira's session check (`validateSession`) applies the same quiet-spell and school sign-in limits as the pages.
- School website designs are listed once in `utils/websiteView.js` (`LAYOUTS`: `group` standard / 3d / emblem, `motion`, `emblem`, `base` for a design drawn on another's page). New designs are behind `website_designs`. The 3D designs move only through `public/js/schoolSiteMotion.js` (transform and opacity, nothing hidden without it, nothing moving for reduced motion); emblems are our own artwork in `utils/websiteEmblems.js`, or the school's logo or image (CSS-safe https links only).
- Uploaded website photos are always shown whole (`views/partials/website/photo.ejs`): they fill their frame only when the shape is close, otherwise they sit over a blurred copy of themselves. Never crop a school's photo with `object-fit: cover` on its own.
- Admissions > Admission Settings is the one place for online admission, student fields and parents & guardians fields (`/common/contact-template` redirects there; each tab follows its own permission). The website page shows online admission as a status line only.
- Emergency & pickup details: each parents & guardians section says who else sees it (`shared_with`: parents, students, teachers, drivers; feature `family_sharing`; `utils/contacts.js`). Parents update only sections shared with parents, on their own record; teachers see the class Pickup list; drivers need `driver_family_details` and see only their own riders.
- School-wide notifications (`recipient_type = 'broadcast'`) reach each member of staff only when they concern them (`utils/notificationAudience.js`): events and announcements go to all staff except drivers (website-only ones go to nobody); a failed email goes to holders of the permission behind it (add each new failure title to `emailFailureRules`), plus managers and supervisors for unlisted ones; any other type goes to managers. For one group of staff, use `notifyByPermission` (the same check as the pages).
- A class's notices go through `classStaff` (`utils/notificationService.js`): never drivers, people on hold or Managira accounts; pass `permission` for the page it links to and `subjectIds` so teachers hear only about subjects they teach. The bell also hides what a person can't act on (`hide` in `broadcastAudience`): drivers never see class work, and assessment notices need Manage Assessments. A student's bell is built when asked (`buildStudentNotifications`): tests they can take now or within two weeks (only with the Testing Room), grades posted and shared, the term's start or end that week, and their birthday.
- The header search (`enrollmentController.searchUsers`) offers a result only to people who can open its page: students with Manage Students, staff with Manage Employees, parents with Manage Guardians, classes and subjects for managers or Manage Classes & Subjects, the school profile with Settings > Profile. Teachers and supervisors see only their own classes' students, parents, classes and subjects; drivers get nothing.
- The contact card (`public/js/contactCard.js`, `public/css/contactCard.css`) is shared by the Mail Room and Parents & guardians; Manage Guardians opens and edits the cards of the school's guardians (`keysInScope`).
- The Mira page only turns Use Mira on or off for each person (`miraUserPermissionsPost`) and keeps their other permissions; everything else is set on the staff form.
- The assessments flow (feature `assessment_flow`, `utils/assessmentApproval.js`): a score saved on the gradebook, or from an online test (`placeOnlineResult`), waits for the teacher's ✓ (`pending_since`); until then parents and the student see `posted_score` / `posted_choice`, and a subject's totals wait too (`visibleScore`, `hideWaitingTotals`). Any new place that shows grades to families uses these. Until the migration `2026-11-12_assessment_flow.sql` has run on a database, `flowOn` is false there and every query keeps to the old columns (`columnsReady`).
- Finished online tests reach parents and the student only once posted, and only as much as the set's `parent_share` allows ('grade' or 'full'; `utils/testResults.js`). Question pictures live in `images/questions/<school>/` (`utils/questionMedia.js`); a student's page never gets a "label the picture" answer.
- Adding an assessment, then its questions, is one flow (`public/js/assessmentFlow.js`): `createTest?flow=1` tells the opener what it made (`managira:assessments-created`), and the questions page opened with `flow=1` says when it's saved or closed.
- Set Assessment Questions is a follow-up of Manage Assessments (every question page and action needs both). Staff never saved keep exactly what they had; only the new-teacher preset adds it, in the assessments flow (`rolePresetPermissions(role, { questions })`).

- A migration's Check and Live steps list the other apps the school ran (library, food service, health office…), read from its lists' names (`utils/migration/apps.js`): what Managira does itself, partly or fully, or keep the app and connect it with a service account and the API areas it needs. Counseling, safety and legal records are never shown as an app.
- The migration's steps switch in place (`public/js/migrationTask.js`): a step is fetched only when it's opened or the pointer rests on its tab, then kept; opening it again shows the kept copy at once (refreshed quietly when older than two minutes, never while someone is typing or uploading), and anything saved, uploaded or finished drops the kept copies. Nothing is fetched ahead on its own. The page reads only what its step shows (lists on Upload, Check and Review; column matches on Match columns, Columns to explain and Review; activity on Review and Go live), and the sandbox summary is kept per import for ten minutes. Its link clicks are handled before the app's loading bar starts, and the bar is stopped when a step shows. Until something is uploaded, every step after Upload is one "Nothing here yet" card (`_blank`): no empty tiles and no ticks. A task that finished well shows only on its own step.
- A migration loads the lists that fit Managira's own features at go-live, recognised by their columns at import (`utils/migration/features.js`, `featureOf`; logs and summaries stay history) and loaded by `utils/migration/loadFeatures.js`: graduates as former students under Graduates & Alumni (graduated on their day: no billing hold, nothing to undo) with their transcripts and documents; buses, drivers, routes, stops in order, weekly runs and riders in Transportation (a dated rider list is the word on who rides; alternate-address days are a detail on the student); a PayGira bill per kind of fee with each student's share and payments, and meal accounts in debt (nothing is sent to families); the parents' master list filling empty phone, email and address; each student's photo as their picture when they have none; graduation year as the built-in group. Nothing the school already has is replaced, every row is in `migration_loaded`, and a rollback (which may take graduates back out: `managira.archive_override`) removes it all. The Check step shows which list goes into which feature.
- Programs & Indicators (`utils/studentFormFields.js` `INDICATORS`, section `programs`): IEP, Section 504, English learner, gifted, meal eligibility, military family, homeless, foster care and migrant as the school's own staff-only student fields, added in one step in Admission Settings › Student fields and filled by a migration (`indicatorFor`). Race and ethnicity, codes and PINs stay restricted details; old IDs and enrollment dates stay read-only.
- A failed read never decides a migration's state: `ready()` and `stateFor` keep their last answer and ask again next time (a busy database once made a school's sandbox look switched off).
- The Setup steps bar is a slim tab on the left edge (as the chat button is on the right), opening out on hover, focus or while its list is open, so it never covers a page.
- The migration's saves (Save, Confirm, Approve, Use…) run in the background (`public/js/migrationTask.js`): only the pressed button says "Saving…", the step is brought up to date in place at the same scroll, and the server's message shows as a small toast. Ticking a student field on or off imports again; where it lives doesn't.
- Each new student field from a migration lives where the school chooses on Match columns (`migration_columns.field_section`, `field_on_application`; suggested by `studentFormFields.suggestHome`): a part of the student form, and the admission form when families should answer it. Race and ethnicity are student fields; codes like a locker combination stay restricted details.
- Meal balances stay in the school's meal app unless it chooses "Move into PayGira" (`migration_jobs.options.mealBalances`): then amounts owed become bills and money in credit each student's PayGira credit (`finance_student_credits`, in the ledger).
- The upload box asks nothing about the files (every list is recognised by itself): files, a .zip, or a whole folder: dragged onto it (no browser question) or chosen with Choose a folder, which uses the browser's folder access where it has it (`showDirectoryPicker`: one plain "view files" question, never Chrome's "Upload N files… only if you trust the site" box that comes with `webkitdirectory`) and the folder picker elsewhere, a compact list with a remove button beside each, Stop while sending, and a word on privacy beside Upload. Reading carries on in the background, and the page says so.
- What schools see never names another platform (no PowerSchool, Skyward, other SIS or third-party app names): "your current system", "your old system", "previous records". Only the help center guides may name them. The request form asks the system as typed text.
- The migration's steps run in order (Upload, Match columns, Columns to explain, Check & import, Staff roles, Review & sign, Go live) and each opens once the one before is done (`stepProgress`, one small read): done ones are ticked, later ones greyed, and a closed step says which comes first. Only approved areas go live (signing needs at least one); after go-live the school can still edit, approve more and Apply again as often as needed (records update, never duplicate). Save buttons stay greyed until something in their form changes; a small "?" explains each part on hover.
- Super Admin › Migration queue › Start over (`startOver`, the school's name typed) empties a sandbox that hasn't gone live (uploads, lists, records, documents, column matches, staff roles, answers) and keeps it open for a fresh upload; the activity log stays and nothing live is touched.
- A migration reads every system's export by the same rules (`utils/migration/powerschool.js`, `lists.js`): a student's number is any of `STUDENT_KEYS` (Student_Number, PermID, Other ID, User ID, person_id, Name-ID…), a field takes the column with its best-known name across the whole list (`bestMatches`: a Mark is the grade, then the Grade beside it is the grade level), and a list is a core list only when its columns fit (`fits`: grades need a course and a mark, parents a name and a way to reach them). A student's other numbers (`ALIAS_KEYS`), a parent's contact number and a family's number reach them from any list (`linkPeople`, `reachOf`, decided by the values); a parents list on its own completes each parent's link to a child; a teacher named rather than numbered is the member of staff of that name; the school's own attendance code list says what each code means (`attendanceCodesOf`) and a day counts once however many period rows it has. Short words in list rules match at a word's start (`ell` never matches "bell").
- Lists a school kept by hand keep working (`utils/migration/forms.js`): on Check & import it ticks which become forms (Managira's suggestion first: per-student lists, not logs a machine wrote, not a family's or a parent's list, at most 60, restricted ones offered but unticked; `setListUse`, `job.options.lists`), and at go-live each is a staff-only Custom editor section that grows (`origin.kind = "old_system"`, kept on every save), every old row an entry on its student dated by its own date, codes and PINs left out (kept restricted read-only) and protected when it holds medical, special education or discipline columns. They sit in a group of their own in the editor and the classroom's Forms tab; Apply again adds only rows it hasn't brought before (`migration_links` `form_entry`); a rollback removes them unless staff have added entries for students who stay. Everything kept is grouped by topic (`topicOf`) on the student's page ("From the old system", forms still in use first, a filter past 8 items), on Check & import and on Records from your old system.
- A migration takes an export as schools really have it (`utils/dataMigration.js`): a student the export says has left (withdrawn, transferred, graduated, passed away, or past their exit date with no active status; PowerSchool's Enroll_Status numbers too) comes in as a former student in Graduates & Alumni with the reason (`leftOf`, archived on the day they left), never in a class; a grade however written ("KG", "PK", "06", "1st Grade") joins the school's own class for that grade before any new one is made (`gradeKey`); a district's export holding several schools waits for the school to choose its own (`setSchools`, a blocker), and nothing about the other schools' students reaches the sandbox (their documents stay on nobody's record, restricted); dates are read in any common form and a list that writes the day first is read day first (`dayFirstColumns`); a number a spreadsheet padded or stripped ("00123", "31000.0") still finds its student (`plainId`); a students list may name them in one column; an email two parents with different first names share in the files makes two people, the first keeping it for signing in; placeholder emails and phones ("n/a", "000-000-0000") are dropped; birthdays a current student can't have are flagged in the check report.
- A student who left before graduating is "Transferred & withdrawn" wherever a person reads it (Graduates & Alumni tab, Graduate & Withdraw, the archive notice, Transcripts' filter); the archive kind stays `left` in code. Admissions' menu group is "Former Students", and long menu labels wrap rather than being cut.
- Once-per-process work a first page needs (tables made if missing, which columns exist) is also started in `logStartupDbStatus` (server.js), side by side, so the first sign-in after a start doesn't wait for it: on a database across the internet (a development server's) each statement is a round trip.
- The help center's "Moving to Managira" guides (`utils/docs/articles/migration.js`) give schools the basics and who to contact (support@managira.com, Contact support, the chat): never how the move works inside (staging, matching, loading, rollback).
